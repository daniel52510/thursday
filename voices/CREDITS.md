# Voice credits

THURSDAY's voice (`thursday.wav`, `thursday.txt`) is cloned at runtime by
Qwen3-TTS from a reference clip made of recordings of speaker **p266**
(female, 22, Irish, Athlone) in the CSTR VCTK Corpus.

> Yamagishi, Junichi; Veaux, Christophe; MacDonald, Kirsten. (2019).
> *CSTR VCTK Corpus: English Multi-speaker Corpus for CSTR Voice Cloning Toolkit (version 0.92)*.
> University of Edinburgh, The Centre for Speech Technology Research (CSTR).
> https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645

Changes: two utterances were trimmed of silence, joined with a short pause,
and stored as a single 16-bit PCM WAV.

THURSDAY's voice is a synthetic clone; it is not, and should not be presented
as, the speaker or any other real person.
